## 3. Title cards: Ink Wash
A Chinese and Japanese ink-painting landscape that paints itself onto rice paper, with a vertical title and a red seal. It suits a calm, story-led opening that should feel crafted rather than digital. Use it for title and chapter cards for travel, food or culture stories.

### The look
- Palette:
  - rice paper #EEEAE0
  - ink #1B1A18
  - one seal red #B8352A
  - The washes are the same black ink at low strength, never a separate grey. Red appears only on the sun, the seal and a couple of tiny accents, such as a bullet square or a sash on the figure.
- Type:
  - The title is Cormorant SC (semibold small caps), tracked wide, about a third of its size, and set vertically top to bottom in two columns.
  - One quiet line in Cormorant Garamond italic, with a small red square in front of it.
  - A small-caps chapter line in warm grey.
- Layout:
  - Three mountain ranges stack from pale to dark, and each fades downward into a band of mist.
  - One side holds a dark dry-brush cliff with a pine and a tiny figure. A small red sun sits just above the far peaks, and a small boat floats on a river of thin broken strokes.
  - The title sits in the empty sky at the top left, with the square seal under it.
  - At least half the frame is empty paper, and the bottom fifth stays calm.
- Texture:
  - Paper fibre and faint grain over everything, including the ink.
  - Ridge lines break up like a dry brush.
  - The sun is slightly mottled with a soft edge.

### How it moves
- Ink spreads, it never fades.
  - Washes bleed in behind a ragged, noisy front: left to right for the mountains, and downward from the ridge for the cliff.
  - A darker wet band trails just behind the front, then dries back to the final tone.
- Strokes grow along their own path, fast then easing out like a brush. The cliff's outline comes first, then dozens of short slanted texture strokes, then the pine's trunk, its branch, and needles flicking out of each cluster.
- The red sun blooms out from its centre with a lumpy, wet edge. It overshoots about 10% and settles soft.
- Text and seal:
  - Text is brushed on with a soft, uneven wipe in reading order.
  - The seal stamps last. It lands at 112% with a few degrees of tilt and settles.
- Frame one already shows paper, pale far peaks and the first ink spreading.
  - Mist and the boat drift slowly the whole time.
  - The finished painting holds for the last second.

### Never
- Opacity fades or crossfades as the reveal. Ink always arrives with an edge.
- Clean digital gradients, hard vector shapes, drop shadows or glow.
- Red used as more than a spot colour, or any second accent colour.
- Text set horizontally across the middle of the landscape.
