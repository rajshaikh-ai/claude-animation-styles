## 7. Product launches: App Mockup
The launch-film product shot: one phone running a real-looking app screen, turning into place on a soft studio backdrop, with a few floating UI cards popping out around it. It suits any video that needs to show a product as if it were already a polished, shipped app. Use it for app launches, SaaS ads, showing a client's product.

### The look
- Palette:
  - soft blue-grey studio backdrop, #dfe6ee at the top fading to #cfd8e2, with a lighter glow #f4f7fa behind the phone and a slightly darker floor
  - near-black ink #0e1116 for numbers and titles, grey #7a818c for labels
  - one product accent, green #0e9f6e, with its pale tint #e3f6ee for chips. Everything else is white.
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans everywhere. ExtraBold for the big numbers with tabular figures, Bold for titles, Medium for body lines, sentence case, slightly tight letter spacing on the numbers.
- Layout:
  - One phone in the middle at a three-quarter angle: tilted back a little, turned about 20 degrees, rolled about 3 degrees, with visible thickness and a metal edge.
  - Its screen is a real app built as UI, not a screenshot: greeting, a big balance, a delta chip, a line chart with a dark value tag, a tab selector, three activity rows and a tab bar.
  - Three white floating cards at different depths: a notification across the top-left corner, a stat card with a mini bar chart on the right edge, a progress-ring card bottom-left. Each overlaps the phone a little and tilts with it.
- Texture: a very fine static grain over the backdrop, a soft floor shadow under the phone, layered soft shadows under the cards, a faint light band across the glass.

### How it moves
- The phone has weight. It is already in frame on the first frame, turned steeply, and swings round to its final angle on a heavy spring: a slight overshoot of a few degrees, then it settles. Its floor shadow widens as it turns to face the camera, and a glint runs along the metal edge mid-turn.
- The screen builds as it lands. The tab pill slides over, the balance counts up on fixed-width digits and eases into the exact figure, the chart draws left to right with a dot riding its tip, the delta chip pops as the count lands, and the activity rows rise in one after another from behind their own edges.
- Every card is a morph and a pop, never a fade. The notification pops out of the phone's camera pill and unfolds into the card. The stat card grows out of its own percentage chip, then its bars spring up, the accent bar last. The progress card starts as a white disc around its ring and stretches into the card while the ring sweeps round.
- One card at a time, about half a second apart, each overshooting about 10% and settling. Then everything floats: a slow camera drift where the nearer cards move more than the phone, plus a gentle bob. Hold the finished frame for the last second.

### Never
- A flat screenshot pasted on a phone, or a phone that slides in at constant speed with no shadow.
- Cards that fade in, or all arrive at once.
- Real brand names, a real phone maker's logo, or a dark backdrop with neon glows.
- Digits that change width while they count.
