## 9. Stories: Timeline
A story told as a walk along the years: a timeline painted on a floor in perspective, with each milestone standing up on its own little card as the line fills toward today. It suits any video that has to cover years in seconds and land on "where we are now". Use it for company stories, "my journey" reels, history explainers.

### The look
- Palette:
  - cool off-white floor #F5F6F8 shading down to #E3E6EB, with a faint grey grid that fades into haze toward the top
  - ink #0E1116 for the headline, card titles and the years already lived; grey #6A717C for the small lines; light grey #B7BEC8 for the stretch of line still to come
  - one electric blue #2442F5 for the filled line, the ticks behind the playhead, the playhead itself and the NOW card. Blue is the only colour.
- Type:
  - Schibsted Grotesk: ExtraBold and tightly spaced for the headline and the big years painted on the floor, Bold for card titles, Medium for the one-line details.
  - JetBrains Mono SemiBold with a little letter spacing for the kicker line and the year on each card.
- Layout:
  - A headline block up top: a tiny logo, a mono kicker ("COMPANY · 2016 TO NOW"), then one line of headline with the payoff phrase in blue.
  - Below it the floor. The line runs from the far top left down to the near bottom middle, with ticks across it and the years painted flat on the floor to its left. Key years are big and black, the in-between years smaller and grey, future years pale.
  - Each milestone is a white card standing on a thin stem from its point on the line: a square thumbnail, the year, a short title, one line. The cards cascade down and to the right, nearer ones slightly bigger.
  - The latest milestone is the NOW card: solid blue, the nearest and biggest, with a small black "NOW" pill on its corner. The playhead dot with a soft ring sits on the line just past it.
- Texture: none. Flat floor, crisp lines, soft layered card shadows.

### How it moves
- The camera walks the years. It opens low and close on the first years, turned a little down the line, then travels toward now in one long eased move: it cranes up, pulls back and swings square, and lands on the final framing about a second after the playhead stops. It never crawls at a steady speed.
- The blue fill and its playhead lead. They are already moving on the first frame, speed up through the middle years and brake into now. Each tick turns blue and its year darkens the moment the playhead crosses it.
- Each milestone is one crisp beat. As the playhead passes its year, the base dot pops, the stem shoots up, and the card hinges up off the stem like a pop-up book, overshooting about 10% before it settles. Leave a clear gap between milestones so a click can sit on each.
- The NOW card lands last with a bit more weight, its pill snaps on a beat later, one ring pings out from the playhead, and then everything holds for the last second.

### Never
- Cards that fade in flat, or a line that fills at one constant speed.
- A second accent colour, gradients on the cards, or a glow on the line.
- A camera still drifting at the end, or a NOW card that lands before the line reaches it.
- Real company names, logos or product photos.
