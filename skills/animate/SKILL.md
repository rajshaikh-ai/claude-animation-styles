# /animate: the style menu

Show the user this table, then ask which style fits their video (one short question). When they pick one, follow that style's skill (for example the `map-route` skill) from its step 1.

| Command | Style | What it makes |
|---|---|---|
| `/map-route` | 3D Map Route | A phone-maps style 3D city where a blue route draws through the streets to a red pin and a trip card pops up |
| `/teardown` | Exploded Teardown | A real 3D product that opens into labelled layers one beat at a time |
| `/ink-wash` | Ink Wash | A Chinese ink-painting landscape that paints itself onto rice paper with a vertical title and red seal |
| `/paper-desk` | Paper Desk | A top-down 'evidence on the desk' explainer: printed sheets land on a green cutting mat and get circled and highlighted |
| `/headline` | Headline Highlight | An invented news article flies in on a 3D tilt, the camera pushes in, a lime highlighter sweeps the key line and a quoted post pops in |
| `/stat-chart` | Stat Chart | A terminal-dark finance graphic: one huge number counts up as a line chart draws and turns orange at the key event |
| `/app-mockup` | App Mockup | A launch-film phone shot: a CSS 3D phone running a real-looking app screen swings into place while floating UI cards pop out |
| `/screen-zoom` | Screen-Recording Zoom | A polished tutorial shot: a dark app window floats on a mint backdrop and the camera zooms in as the cursor glides to a button and clicks |
| `/timeline` | Timeline | A timeline painted on a 3D floor: the camera walks the years while milestone cards hinge up and the blue NOW card lands last |

Recommend by what the video is about: places -> /map-route, a product's insides -> /teardown, a calm story opening -> /ink-wash, making a case with evidence -> /paper-desk, backing a claim with a source -> /headline, a result or growth number -> /stat-chart, showing an app -> /app-mockup, a click-by-click tutorial -> /screen-zoom, a journey over years -> /timeline.

Each command takes the user's words after it, e.g. `/map-route Tip Top Cafe 12 min 3.4 km`, and can cut the clip into a video they attach.
